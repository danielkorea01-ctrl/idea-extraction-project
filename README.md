# idea-extraction-project
An open, independent investigation into how AI platforms may extract, aggregate, and monetize user-generated ideas, and the implications for intellectual ownership, privacy, and digital autonomy.

**Version 0.1 | 17 October 2026 | Exploratory research proposal**

This release separates published research, testable hypotheses, and unverified assumptions. It presents no finding of misconduct by any company and no original experimental results.

## Idea

**Hypothesis:** AI conversations could be transformed into aggregated product intelligence. This project asks how such a process could work and how user contributions could be traced, acknowledged, or voluntarily rewarded.

## Rationale

Published work describes semantic sentence representations and document topic clustering. These are candidate components, not evidence that a provider deploys the proposed pipeline. See [sources and their limits](docs/sources.md).

## Evidence Gap

We have no direct evidence establishing the proposed covert pipeline at a named provider, or the unauthorized commercial use of a specific user's idea. A similar product appearing after a conversation does not establish causation.

## Conclusion

The first research task is to test technical capability and define what evidence would distinguish documented practice from speculation. Questions about consent, provenance, and value distribution remain open.
